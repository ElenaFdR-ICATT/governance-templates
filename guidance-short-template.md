This guidance follows the headings in the template.
Quotes from the template are inset.

The guidance provides extra context for the choices and logic of the template.

This should help:

* as you complete it
* to explain it to other people in your community


## Introduction

> [Codebase] 

Start by doing a search and replace for [codebase] to customize this for your project.

> [public organizations]

Listing commissioning public organizations first is deliberate: it gives public recognition for the original visionaries/funders. Hopefully this recognition will make it easier for them to move on from dominating leadership if that’s better for the community.

> It follows [pro-open source public policy, reference architecture or coding guidance] [developed/recommended/required] by [issuing public authority].

Set out the public policy context this project conforms to. Especially helpful if the policies make it easier for new public orgs in the same regulatory context to adopt. 

Depending on how valuable/unique this is, also put into marketing and the README.

>  two steering teams, a product team and technical team

Deliberate choice for the word ‘team’ over ‘committee’. Why? It sounds more action focused than bureaucratic, it’s easier for non-native English speakers to read quickly (lower reading level), and will come laden with fewer associations from other contexts (eg national parliamentary committees known for partisan politics or delaying initiatives).
